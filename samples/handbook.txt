Taller Norte remote-work handbook

People may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Tuesday is an in-office day for
every team, because that is when planning and design reviews happen.

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the Europe/Madrid time zone. Outside that
window, people may shift their schedule as long as they attend the meetings on
their calendar.

The equipment stipend is 500 USD every two years. It covers a chair, a monitor,
or a keyboard. Submit the receipt to finance within 30 days of the purchase.

Visitors are not allowed on remote-work days unless the office manager confirms
a desk the day before.
